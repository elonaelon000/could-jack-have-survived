# Titanic train.csv — provenance and scope

- Dataset: Kaggle Titanic — Machine Learning from Disaster competition: https://www.kaggle.com/c/titanic/data
- Source of this local copy: user-provided `train.csv`, uploaded October 12, 2026.
- Shape: **891 rows, 12 columns**; includes the observed `Survived` outcome.
- The original CSV has been preserved without modifying values or rows.
- SHA-256 of the uploaded file, with its original CRLF newline style: `7d118fef8b6ccf7f81111877bc388536f7b1e498a655e3d649d19aaa010e9f6f`.
- The official Kaggle training sample is not the complete Titanic passenger-and-crew population. No individual lifeboat assignment or evacuation-time record is provided.

Do not replace with `test.csv` or `gender_submission.csv` because they do not provide actual survival outcome labels for our analyses.
